# Five-minute local walkthrough

1. Run `npm ci` then `npm run demo`. Open `/` to see the marketing reference. The two audience proof points distinguish cumulative event attendance from newsletter subscribers.
2. Open `/tokyo`. Browse the six preview concepts and their category/neighborhood controls. The green demo strip makes fictional data and simulated payments explicit.
3. Choose **Maya · All Access** in the account selector. All 24 sample places become available. Save a place, browse people, and create a Right now coffee invitation.
4. Send **Alex** a connection request from People. Choose **Alex · Preview member**, open the bell/Connections page, and accept. Sample contacts appear only after acceptance. Neither member needs paid access to revisit an accepted connection.
5. As Alex, open Membership → Try a demo purchase → Complete demo purchase. The app writes an invoice, verifies a simulated settlement and grants global access. Refresh the page and inspect invoice history. A renewal adds thirty days after the current paid-through date.
6. Open Settings. Hide the profile, remove a stored contact, or review blocked members. ENS controls stay unavailable without a real configured Sepolia integration; no fake name appears.
7. Choose **Host · Admin** and open Content & operations. Inspect the place editor, moderation and invoice states. There is no manual “mark paid” control.

Before a real hackathon recording, replace the sample content and complete the live integrations listed in INTEGRATIONS.md. This local walkthrough proves application behavior; it is not mainnet or ENSv2 transaction evidence.
